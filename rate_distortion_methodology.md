# Rate--distortion methodology

## Corpus and transforms

The frozen corpus contains all Kodak PNGs under
`datasets/kodak/PhotoCD_PCD0992` plus the first ten DIV2K validation LR images
under `datasets/div2k/DIV2K_valid_LR_bicubic/X2` (34 source images total).
Each source is converted to RGB and resized with Pillow Lanczos to 512x512 and
1024x1024 before the timed CAPS call. The source RGB bytes are resident before
the codec timer starts. `rate_distortion_results.csv` contains the full sweep:
qualities 100, 90, 82, 70, 50, 35, 20, 32-pixel tiles, and a 64-pixel adaptive
tile comparison at quality 82 (544 rows).

The reported stream byte count is the complete CAPS vector: 64-byte header,
40-byte entries for every chunk, payloads, and checksums. No side table or
external model is omitted. Progressive decoding is measured separately in
`progressive_results.csv`.

## Metrics and constraints

PSNR is computed over all RGB samples. The MS-SSIM column is a deterministic
four-scale geometric-mean SSIM proxy (per-channel means, variances, and
covariance; 2x box reduction between scales). It is explicitly named
`ms_ssim_proxy`; it is not a substitute for a learned metric.

LPIPS was audited as unavailable on the CPU-only reference environment, so all
rows carry `lpips_status=unavailable` and `constraint_status=unverified_lpips`.
The A/B/C LPIPS thresholds therefore remain acceptance targets rather than
claims. A future external LPIPS audit can join on image, size, quality, and
tile without changing the encoded streams.

PNG, JPEG, WebP, and AVIF baselines are generated by `scripts/dataset_benchmark.py`.
JXL availability is recorded explicitly in `baseline_availability.csv`; no JXL
encoder was installed on the reference host. Baseline settings are JPEG/WebP
quality 82 and AVIF libsvtav1 CRF 30. PNG is lossless and is not a quality
search point.

## Search and reporting rules

Every candidate is rejected if its complexity estimate cannot plausibly fit the
2.5-second gate. CAPS performs analytical lifting, two post-pyramid coefficient
passes, and independent tile emission with eight workers. No local optimization
iterations are used. Results are retained when they add a non-dominated
byte/quality point, remove overhead without changing quality, or improve speed
without a quality regression. A result that only wins on one corpus is labeled
`DATASET-SPECIFIC`; a metric unavailable to the harness is never inferred.
