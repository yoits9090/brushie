# Benchmark material provenance

The codec does not require benchmark images to build or run. The checked-out images are retained only as a research snapshot; verify upstream terms before redistributing this repository or derived artifacts.

- **Kodak Lossless True Color Image Suite:** copies used here were mirrored from [MohamedBakrAli/Kodak-Lossless-True-Color-Image-Suite](https://github.com/MohamedBakrAli/Kodak-Lossless-True-Color-Image-Suite), which points to the original Kodak test suite.
- **DIV2K:** evaluation uses official validation material from [ETH CVL](https://data.vision.ee.ethz.ch/cvl/DIV2K/). DIV2K is ignored by Git and must be obtained under its upstream terms.
- **USC-SIPI:** `datasets/public/usc_sipi` comes from the [USC-SIPI Image Database](https://sipi.usc.edu/database/). Consult its current terms before redistribution.

Benchmark CSVs and images are research artifacts, not part of the codec's MIT software license. If you cannot redistribute a dataset under its applicable terms, remove it from a distribution and regenerate outputs from a lawful local checkout. Old manifests may contain machine-local paths and are not portable inputs.
