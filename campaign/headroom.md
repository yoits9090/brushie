## Implications for the 2x target (4,700 / 9,300 / 21,800 B)

1. **The detail-band entropy coder IS the codec.** Cutting luma detail bytes
   in half at equal quality would alone reach the moonshot. Current coder:
   causal 8-state significance context + sign context + unary quotient with
   per-position contexts + local Rice k (per-coefficient from neighbor
   magnitudes, re-adapted every 64 magnitudes), binary range coder with
   11-bit adaptive probabilities.
2. Detail coefficients at these gates are mostly |q|<=2 (unary quotient 0-1),
   so the sig+sign bits dominate the tail; the local-k magnitude model only
   compresses the head. Expected wins: energy-bucket contexts (AUREA-style
   64-128 classes), tANS/rANS with exact probabilities, bitplane/refinement
   passes at high quality, and better spatial prediction of significance.
3. Base LL (15% at low quality, ~2 KB) is where the chat/meme gap lives:
   flat/polynomial patches + run-lengths attack this directly.
4. Chroma detail grows with quality (7->12%) - chroma-from-luma prediction
   (geom-lab) can cut it; coder gains apply to all bands.
